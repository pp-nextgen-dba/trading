Supply & demand chain

Note: Tesla also designs its own AI training and inference silicon (Dojo, AI5/AI6) in-house, alongside buying NVIDIA GPUs — so its NVIDIA reliance may shrink over time as that ramps. SpaceX's tie to this chain runs through its 2026 acquisition of xAI, whose Colossus GPU cluster is built on NVIDIA hardware; SpaceX (Nasdaq: SPCX) IPO'd on Nasdaq in June 2026. Apple's AI server chip ("Baltra") is being co-developed with Broadcom, with production targeted for 2027 under a partnership extended through 2031. Microchip supplies PCIe/CXL interconnect and power-control silicon that data centers need regardless of which GPU vendor they buy from.
